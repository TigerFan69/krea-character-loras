Read LICENSE.pdf, NOTICE.txt and CREATOR-TERMS.md in the download. Obtain compatible Krea 2 base-model components separately under their terms. Place `krea2_erikachar_49188442b90f.safetensors` in `ComfyUI/models/loras/`, refresh and select it in a compatible Krea 2 workflow. Trigger: **erikachar**.
